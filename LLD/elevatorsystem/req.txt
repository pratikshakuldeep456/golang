Filter to eligible elevators. An elevator is eligible if:

It's idle (not moving) → always eligible
It's moving UP and currently at or below floor 6 → eligible, 
 -  since it'll pass floor 6 on the way and can pick up without detour
It's moving DOWN → not eligible right now 
 — even if it's close, it's moving away first and would need to fully reverse before reaching floor 6, which usually costs more time than picking a different elevator
It's moving UP but already past floor 6 (e.g., elevator at floor 8, moving up) 
   → not eligible for this trip; it'd have to finish its sweep, reverse, and come back down — treat it like a "moving away" case

Among eligible elevators, pick by distance. Simplest metric: abs(elevator.CurrentFloor - requestFloor).
