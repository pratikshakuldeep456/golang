Your parking spot only needed a single true/false — perfect fit for atomic.Bool's compare-and-swap. But a movie seat now needs to track multiple related fields together — Status (enum: AVAILABLE/RESERVED/BOOKED), ReservedAt (timestamp), BookingID (which booking holds it). atomic.Bool can only atomically guard one value;
 the moment you need to check-and-update several fields as a single atomic unit,
  you need a proper mutex around the whole struct (or the whole seat map), 
  not just a lock-free primitive on one field.
