One store — we don't need multi-vendor/store handling.
Store has inventory for each product.
Each product can have its own purchase quantity limit oon each order.
Example: iPhone → max 2/user
Shirt → max 10/user
Some products → no limit.
Purchase limit is per user per product.
The limit applies to total purchased quantity + current cart quantity.
Inventory is also checked during cart/checkout.
Checkout must be atomic — either the complete order succeeds or nothing changes.
Multiple users can purchase the same product concurrently.
We need to prevent a user from exceeding the product's limit due to concurrent checkout requests.
